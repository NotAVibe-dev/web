Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

notavibe connects the companies that depend on open source with the maintainers who keep it alive, matched to real usage. This explains what personal data we handle, why, and the rights you have. Any question or request: hello@notavibe.dev — we are the data controller.

What we collect, and why

  • Early-access sign-ups. If you join the waitlist, we store your email address and whether you identified as a maintainer or a backer — used only to tell you when notavibe launches. Legal basis: your consent. You can opt out at any time, permanently.
  • Open-source maintainer information. To match funding to real usage, we process professional information about maintainers — name, a public/professional email, platform handles, and bio — collected only from public sources (a maintainer’s public GitHub profile, npm-published metadata, or their own funding/website page). We never harvest emails from commit logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in connecting maintainers with funding, balanced against your rights.
  • WhatsApp infra bot (internal). A private bot lets our own team open infrastructure requests over WhatsApp. It processes the sender’s WhatsApp number and the content of the messages they send it; each request is recorded as an issue in our private GitHub repository and kept in that issue history. Access is limited to an allow-list of our own team, and our operational logs record only handles and outcomes — never phone numbers or message contents. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating our own infrastructure.
  • Website analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless — it does not track you across sites or build a profile of you.

We do not collect special-category data, and we don’t use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Who we share it with

We use a small set of processors to run the service, each under a data-processing agreement: Google (Workspace and Cloud), GitHub (source and data), and Cloudflare (DNS, content delivery/security, and analytics). The site is served through Cloudflare’s edge network, which transiently processes connection data (including your IP address) to deliver and protect it — legal basis: our legitimate interest in secure, reliable delivery. Where these providers transfer data internationally, that transfer is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist email: until notavibe launches or you opt out, whichever comes first.
  • Maintainer information: until you opt out or it is no longer relevant. Opt-outs are permanent — once you ask to be removed, we keep a minimal suppression record so we don’t re-add you.
  • Infra-bot requests: kept in our private issue history for as long as they are operationally useful.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, or object to our processing. To exercise any of these, email hello@notavibe.dev and we’ll action it — if a maintainer asks to be removed, we delete the record and add it to a permanent suppression list the same day. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Changes

We’ll update this policy as notavibe takes shape; the “last updated” date above always reflects the current version.
