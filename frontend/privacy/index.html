Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data notavibe handles when you visit notavibe.dev, why we handle it, who else touches it, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over it. notavibe has not launched: this site is a description of what we are building, plus a form to be told when it is ready. When the product itself starts processing personal data, this policy will be updated before that happens, not after.

1.Who we are

notavibe connects the companies that depend on open source with the maintainers who keep it alive, matched to real usage. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR, notavibe is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

You can reach us about anything in this document at hello@notavibe.dev. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as we are not required to; that address reaches the people who can action your request directly.

2.What we collect, and why

Four things, and only while you are on this site. Each is listed with the lawful basis we rely on for it.

2.1 Waitlist sign-ups

If you give us your email address to be told when notavibe launches, we store that address and nothing else alongside it. We use it for that one announcement. We do not use it for unrelated marketing, we do not pass it to anyone else, and you can withdraw your consent and be removed at any time, permanently.

Lawful basis: consent (Article 6(1)(a)).

2.2 Server and CDN request logs

Serving a page necessarily involves receiving a request for it. Our content delivery network records the metadata it needs in order to serve and protect the site — your IP address, the time of the request, the URL requested, your user agent and the referring page. We see this only as aggregate traffic figures.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — secure, reliable delivery of the site.

2.3 Website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It is cookieless: it stores nothing on your device, it does not fingerprint you, it does not follow you between websites, and it does not build a profile of you. It reports page views, referring sites, and coarse technical details such as browser, operating system and country, together with page performance measurements.

It works through a small script that Cloudflare adds to our pages at its edge, served from static.cloudflareinsights.com. There is no advertising network involved and no data broker; the measurements are visible only to us.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — understanding whether the site is read and whether it performs.

2.4 Web fonts

The typefaces on these pages are served by Google Fonts rather than by us, which means your browser requests those font files from Google directly and Google receives your IP address and user agent as a consequence. We set no cookie for it and we send Google no information about you beyond what any request for a file necessarily reveals.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — presenting a consistent, legible site.

3.What we do not collect

We do not collect special-category data as defined in Article 9 of the GDPR — nothing about health, ethnicity, religion, political opinions, sexual orientation, trade union membership, genetics or biometrics. We do not carry out profiling or automated decision-making. We do not sell personal data, and we have never sold personal data.

There are no accounts on this site, so there is nothing to sign in to and no profile attached to you. notavibe is a tool for professional software developers and the organisations that employ them; it is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.

4.Cookies and local storage

We set no cookies of our own — none for advertising and none for cross-site tracking. Our analytics is cookieless and stores nothing on your device, which is why there is no cookie banner on this site: there is nothing to consent to.

If you use the light/dark theme switch, your choice is saved in your own browser's local storage under the key nv-theme. That is not a cookie, it is never transmitted to us, and clearing your browser storage removes it.

Our content delivery network may set a strictly necessary cookie of its own for bot detection and security. We do not control its contents and we do not read it.

5.Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. We do use a small number of service providers to run this site, each of them acting as a processor under an agreement with us:

ProviderWhat it does for us
Cloudflare DNS, content delivery, security, and the cookieless website analytics described in section 2.3. This site is served through Cloudflare's edge network, which transiently processes connection data — including your IP address — to deliver and protect it.
GitHub Hosts this site's files and serves them.
Google Workspace, for the inbox that receives anything you send us. Google Fonts separately serves the typefaces on these pages.

We may also disclose personal data where we are legally required to — for example in response to a valid legal request — or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

6.International transfers

Some of the providers above process data outside the European Economic Area, principally in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, together with the additional safeguards those mechanisms require.

7.How long we keep it

DataRetention period
Waitlist email Until notavibe launches and we have told you, or until you opt out — whichever comes first. Then deleted.
Request logs Retained by our content delivery network under its own retention schedule. We keep no separate copy and build no history of your visits.
Analytics Retained by Cloudflare under its own retention schedule, as aggregate counts rather than records about individuals. We keep no separate copy.
Theme preference Stays in your browser until you clear its storage. We never receive it.

8.Your rights

If the GDPR applies to you, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy of it.
  • Rectify it, if it is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase it, in the circumstances set out in Article 17.
  • Restrict how we process it, in the circumstances set out in Article 18.
  • Port it — receive it in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis we rely on.

To exercise any of these, email hello@notavibe.dev. We will respond within one month, as the GDPR requires. There is no form to complete and no account to create, and exercising a right will never cost you anything.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — normally the data protection authority of the country where you live or work — at any time, and without contacting us first.

9.Changes to this policy

We will update this policy as notavibe develops, and we will do so before any new processing begins rather than after. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the version you are reading. Because this site is maintained in a public repository, the complete history of every change to this page is public in NotAVibe-dev/web.
